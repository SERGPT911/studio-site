FAQ

Questions clients ask first.

Something else? Ask us directly.

How long does a typical project take?

A landing page usually takes 5–7 days, a business website 2–4 weeks, an automation or bot 1–3 weeks, and an AI assistant 2–4 weeks. Larger products are split into milestones of 2–3 weeks each. The exact timeline is in the written proposal before you pay anything.

How do payments work?

We work by invoice. You can pay by international bank transfer or through Mellow, which handles contracts and payments for remote contractors. Typical terms are 50% upfront and 50% on delivery; larger projects are paid per milestone.

Do you communicate in English?

Yes — all communication, documentation and code comments are in English. We work async by email, Telegram or Slack, with calls when they're useful.

Who owns the code and accounts?

You do. Code goes into your repository, services are registered to your accounts, and you get all credentials and handover docs at launch.

What happens after launch?

30 days of bug fixes are included. After that we offer monthly support or pay-as-you-go changes — your choice, no lock-in.
